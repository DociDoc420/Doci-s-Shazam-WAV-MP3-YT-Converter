# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in **Doci's Song Converter**! 🎧

This is a **closed-source** application, so we don't accept code contributions or pull requests. But there are great ways to help:

- 🐛 **Report a bug** — open a thread in [Discussions](../../discussions) with your Windows version and what happened.
- 💡 **Request a feature** — tell us what would make your DJ prep faster.
- ⭐ **Star the repo** and **share the app** with other DJs.
- 💬 **Help other users** in Discussions.

For anything else, reach us on Telegram: **[@PTNPNX105](https://t.me/PTNPNX105)**

Please keep it friendly — see our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
