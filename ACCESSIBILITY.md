# Accessibility

We want **Doci's Song Converter** to be usable by as many DJs as possible, including those who rely on assistive technology. Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and this page is an honest snapshot of where things stand — not a finished promise.

## Our commitment
- We treat accessibility barriers as real bugs. If something blocks you, [tell us](#reporting-a-barrier) and we'll prioritize a fix.
- We aim for clear text, readable contrast, and keyboard-operable controls.
- We keep this page updated as the app improves.

## Supported environments
- **Windows 10 and Windows 11** (64-bit), desktop.
- Works with the operating system's **display scaling** and larger text settings.
- Mouse and **keyboard** navigation (Tab / Enter / Esc) across the main actions.
- An internet connection is required for the core convert feature.

## Known limitations
We'd rather be upfront about these:
- **Screen readers:** the app has not yet been fully tested with NVDA, JAWS, or Narrator. Some controls may not announce their labels correctly.
- **Desktop only:** there is currently no mobile or web version, so phone-based accessibility tools don't apply.
- **Color:** some status is shown with color (e.g. green = done, red = error); we're adding text/icon labels so color isn't the only cue.
- **Captions/audio:** the app plays and processes audio but doesn't depend on hearing it to operate.
- **No offline mode:** converting requires being online.

We're actively working to reduce these.

## Reporting a barrier
If any part of the app is hard or impossible to use, please let us know — it genuinely helps us improve:
- Open a thread in [Discussions](../../discussions), **or**
- Message us on Telegram: **[@ShazamYoutubeYTConvert_bot](https://t.me/ShazamYoutubeYTConvert_bot)**

Tell us what you were trying to do, what got in the way, and which assistive technology (if any) you use. We'll follow up.
